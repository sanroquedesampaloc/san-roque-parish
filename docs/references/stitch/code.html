Sunday Mass October 5, 2026

The Virtue of Patient Healing: Emulating St. Roch in Times of Trial

By Rev. Fr. Parish Priest, SVD

"The wound of the pilgrim is not merely a mark of affliction, but the very vessel through which God pours forth His consoling balm upon the world."

In our weekly reflection, we contemplate how the holy patron of Sampaloc chose solitary exile rather than spreading affliction, only to find divine providence in the wilderness. Today, when moral and spiritual afflictions test our parish families, we are called to bring the healing presence of Christ to our neighbors.
