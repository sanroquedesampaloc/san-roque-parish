## Brand & Style

This design system translates centuries of Roman Catholic liturgical heritage, baroque gilded metalwork, and sacred vestment traditions into an elevated digital medium. Crafted for ecclesiastical institutions, basilicas, religious archives, and commemorative liturgical events, the interface balances solemn reverence with radiant ceremonial majesty.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily on **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Elegance** fused with **Classical Editorial Typography**. The visual tone evokes sensory impressions of hand-hammered reliquaries, polished brass ostensoria, gold-leaf fleur-de-lis flourishes, and heavy velvet folds bathed in soft candlelight.

Key emotional responses intended by this system:
- **Reverence & Sanctity:** Evoking a contemplative, sacred pause through warm, quiet parchment backdrops and measured vertical proportions.
- **Ecclesiastical Heritage:** Anchored in classical Roman lapidary inscriptions, historical typography, and baroque filigree.
- **Radiant Dignity:** Expressed through gilded metallic strokes, luminous highlights, and rich liturgical burgundy.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the sacred composition: the deep crimson-wine of liturgical velvet, the burnished brilliance of the reliquary’s gilded gold, and the soft, glowing warmth of aged vellum and altar linens.

### Primary Color: Liturgical Burgundy (`#500c14`)
A solemn, deep sacramental crimson evoking ecclesiastical garments, velvet altar reredos, and ancient leather bindings. Used for primary headlines, authoritative display lettering, primary button fills, and critical structural anchors.

### Secondary Color: Gilded Gold (`#c59a3f`)
A lustrous, warm metallic gold extracted from baroque ormolu metalwork and gilded filigree. Applied to decorative hairline dividers, fleur-de-lis motifs, button borders, badge frames, and luminous focal accents. When rendered in gradients, it shifts smoothly from `#e8cb76` through `#c59a3f` to `#8e651e`.

### Tertiary Color: Imperial Crimson (`#8c1d28`)
A richer, warmer red tone that provides depth and gradient shifts when paired with Liturgical Burgundy. Used for badge fills, interactive hover states, selected tab indicators, and critical date banners.

### Neutral Palette: Sacred Parchment & Incense Amber
- **Canvas Base (`#f5ebd7`):** Warm aged champagne parchment that replaces cold modern whites, eliminating visual glare and establishing a warm, illuminated ambiance.
- **Surface Elevation (`#fcf7ed`):** Light creamy vellum for raised card surfaces and content wells.
- **Surface Dark / Crypt (`#1e0508`):** Deep mahogany-black reserved for high-contrast liturgical footers, header strips, and solemn modal backdrops.
- **Text Neutral On-Light (`#281113`):** Deep sepia-charcoal ensuring WCAG AAA legibility across parchment grounds without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is grounded in classical lapidary stone inscriptions and continental book arts:

- **Display & Headings (EB Garamond):** Possesses refined serifs, graceful chiseled proportions, and dramatic uppercase presence reminiscent of monumental Roman engravings. Display headings are styled in uppercase with intentional, generous tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`).
- **Body Text (Libre Caslon Text):** Provides exceptional rhythm, readability, and scholarly gravitas for continuous devotional prayers, litanies, and descriptive chronicles.
- **Accents & Prepositions (EB Garamond Italic):** Connective words like *"of"*, *"the"*, and liturgical versicles use the fluid, swash-inspired italics of EB Garamond, rendered in secondary gilded gold (`#c59a3f`) to provide a lyrical counterpoint to austere uppercase titles.
- **Labels & Banners:** Set exclusively in small capitals with elevated tracking (`0.15em` - `0.2em`) to echo inscribed bronze plaques and bookplate headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an architectural, contemplative model that balances formal axial symmetry with classical asymmetrical counterpoints (such as an off-center venerated relic mirrored against regal typography).

### Grid Model
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Max content container is capped at `1200px` to maintain an intimate manuscript column width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows generous vertical pauses (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between ceremonial sections, creating visual breathing room akin to cathedral naves. Decorative filigree rules and fleur-de-lis motifs always preserve a minimum of `space-md` clearance above and below text boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of sacramental luminosity, gilded edge highlights, and velvety atmospheric depth:

### Gilded Luminescence
Surfaces do not float; they rest like precious materials on an altar. Elevation is achieved through soft, candlelight-tinted radial glows (`rgba(224, 185, 102, 0.18)` blur 32px) and warm ambient backdrops that mimic light radiating behind a reliquary ostensorium.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Sanctuary Floor):** Canvas parchment gradient (`#f5ebd7` to `#ecdcc0`) or deep sanctuary velvet (`#2d070b` to `#4b0d14`).
- **Tier 1 (Reliquary Plinth):** High-grade card elements rendered in `#fcf7ed` with a delicate `1px` border of burnished gold (`rgba(197, 154, 63, 0.45)`).
- **Tier 2 (Floating Cartouche / Modal):** Layered banners featuring a multi-stop box shadow: an ambient warm umber wash (`rgba(40, 17, 19, 0.12)` 0 8px 24px) paired with an inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 238, 180, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs architectural, classical contours. Curvature is kept subtle (`roundedness: 1` = 0.25rem base radius) to evoke carved stone altars, parchment certificates, and beveled brass labels.

### Distinctive Geometric Treatments
- **Notched Cartouche & Chamfered Labels:** Primary badges and date banners feature clipped 45-degree chamfers or inverted bracket cuts (`clip-path` or custom SVG frames) mirroring heraldic shields and reliquary pedestals.
- **Borders & Filigree Rules:** Borders utilize delicate dual-line treatments—a thin outer `1px` gold rule enclosing an inner hairline rule with a centered fleur-de-lis or cross terminal.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Ceremonial Button:** Deep burgundy base (`#500c14`) encased in a `1px` burnished gold border (`#c59a3f`). Text is set in Garamond Small Caps with `0.15em` letter-spacing in soft parchment white (`#fefaf2`). Hover states introduce a subtle golden amber inner illumination (`rgba(220, 175, 75, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Gilded Outline Button:** Transparent background framed by a `1.5px` antique gold border (`#c59a3f`) with deep burgundy typography. Hover state transitions the background to warm gold leaf (`rgba(197, 154, 63, 0.1)`).

### Cartouche Date & Status Badges
- Modeled directly after the commemorative date plaque: an elongated hexagon or chamfered lozenge filled with imperial crimson (`#500c14`), surrounded by a gilded double border (`#c59a3f`), bearing small-caps typography highlighted with faint top-and-bottom gold pinpoint glints.

### Cards & Parchment Panels
- Crafted on `#fcf7ed` with a soft paper tooth texture effect. Edged with a single `1px` gold stroke (`#d8b86d`). Internal padding adheres to `space-lg`. Corner terminals may feature subtle ornamental Victorian or Baroque leaf accents.

### Form Inputs & Select Fields
- Subtle cream backgrounds (`#f8f2e6`) with inset sepia shadow hints. Borders sit at `1px` solid `#d5be9b`. Upon focus, the border shifts to Gilded Gold (`#c59a3f`) with an accompanying warm halo (`rgba(197, 154, 63, 0.2)`). Labels remain strictly serif small capitals positioned above fields.

### Checkboxes & Radio Indicators
- Custom square checkboxes with `0.125rem` corners and gold frames. Selected states reveal a gilded Maltese cross or classical checkmark in deep burgundy. Radio options feature concentric gold rings enclosing a velvet crimson center dot.

### Dividers & Section Accents
- Horizontal dividers consist of a center-tapered `1px` gold hairline interrupted at the midpoint by an ornamental sacred motif (fleur-de-lis, cloverleaf, or four-point cross star) flanked by dual decorative beads.
